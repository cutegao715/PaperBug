# Journal and conference requirements

Use the workspace manuscript profile: target, journal/conference, article type, track, publication stage and language. Reuse current verified sources where applicable. No target venue is needed for a local language edit.

Separate scientific fit, official requirements, examples from published papers and author/lab preferences. Store each consequential rule with its classification, source URL or imported instructions, checked date and applicable artifact/stage. A date or a user-adopted record does not establish independent verification; inspect the applicable source before claiming compliance. Venue changes require reassessment of old rules, not automatic reuse.

For a journal, check relevant author instructions for article type, abstract format, length, figures, supplementary material, declarations, reference style and submission files. Published examples illustrate practice, not necessarily the current rules. A fit recommendation does not predict acceptance.

For a conference, first identify the actual edition and track. When relevant, verify: abstract versus paper deadlines and timezone; page limits and what counts; anonymization in prose, acknowledgments, filenames, metadata and links; template; appendices and supplementary files; rebuttal length and allowed content; artifact policies; and camera-ready requirements. These are possible requirements, not universal mandates.

Keep anonymous submission and author-identified final versions distinguishable. Do not delete author information from the only source copy. A conference rebuttal is not automatically a journal-style revision: only promise or upload changes permitted for that round, and never claim an unperformed experiment is complete.

At camera-ready or proofs, preserve accepted scientific content and respect the actual allowed change scope. Presentation/slides/poster are separate artifacts when requested, not prerequisites for completing a paragraph edit.

If requirements are unavailable or contradictory, record the unresolved source and continue applicable local work without marking the package compliant. Do not submit externally or change author identities merely because this guide was loaded.
