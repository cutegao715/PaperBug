# Methods, results and evidence

Read relevant manuscript argument records and source evidence, not merely an older summary. Record IDs and draft hashes locate evidence; they do not prove that an analysis is sound.

Choose checks by declared study design and actual claims:

| Design | Questions worth checking when relevant |
| --- | --- |
| Observational/survey | Sampling and observation units, measurement, missingness, dependence, estimand, effect sizes and uncertainty |
| Experiment/quasi-experiment | Allocation, controls, outcome timing, identification, attrition, treatment versus mechanism claims |
| Qualitative/mixed | Sampling logic, coding, quotations, researcher role, negative cases, integration and transfer boundaries |
| Computational/simulation | Inputs, assumptions, algorithms, calibration versus validation, scenarios, replications, baselines and sensitivity |
| AI/LLM | Model/version/date, prompts/context, selection of runs, failures, aggregation, leakage and input-conditioned claims |
| Review/synthesis | Search and screening basis, included sources, extraction, quality, heterogeneity and limits of coverage |
| Theory/method/data | Premises or requirements, derivation/design, appropriate demonstration/evaluation and use boundaries |

These are diagnostic questions, not proof that missing information invalidates a study or authorization to run new analyses.

Separate a parameter's origin, assumptions about applying it, and evidence that its application was validated. A literature citation may justify an assumption without validating the chosen numerical value.

Build Results from actual outputs. Identify the comparator, unit, denominator, time window, sample and uncertainty whenever they affect meaning. State the substantive finding with the necessary values; do not just list statistics or force a “so what” sentence after every figure. Locate methods with no reported output and results whose method is unexplained.

Distinguish statistical significance from practical importance; nonsignificance from equivalence; correlation/prediction from causation; model fit from mechanism validity; simulated from observed behavior. Separate significance in two groups is not itself a test of their difference. Missing provenance is a question to resolve, not permission to fabricate a correction.

Use supported explanations in the relevant Results passage. Keep plausible but untested mechanisms explicitly qualified, normally in Discussion. A programmed model rule does not alone establish why an aggregate result occurred.

When numbers or definitions change, identify affected plots, captions, methods, interpretations and summaries. Request or inspect the actual output as needed; never generate missing values to make the manuscript look complete.
