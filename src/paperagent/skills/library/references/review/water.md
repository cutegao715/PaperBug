# Water resources, coupled systems and uncertainty

Use only for substantive water-resources, policy, coupled natural–human systems or related uncertainty claims. A study-area mention does not establish this review standard.

Identify natural, human and institutional entities; variables, decisions, feedbacks, exchanged information and spatial/temporal scales. Distinguish one-way forcing, bidirectional feedback and real-time exchange. The gap can be conceptual, empirical, methodological or integration-related; it need not be a modeling gap.

For policy comparisons inspect actual definitions, jurisdictional sources, baseline, timing, compliance assumptions and distributional effects. Distinguish policy design, modeled implementation, scenario results and observed feasibility. A statement such as mandatory or approved needs an applicable source, not inference from a model label.

For frameworks and tools inspect requirements, alternatives, interoperability, reuse, evaluation and adoption boundaries. Preserve conceptual or integration contributions when supported; do not require a fixed list of competing approaches.

For uncertainty inspect definitions, data/parameter/structural sources, identifiability, calibration, sensitivity and propagation. Scenario spread is not automatically a probabilistic prediction; equifinality is not empirical equivalence. Match verbs such as characterize, quantify or reduce to the actual analysis.

For reviews inspect source selection, synthesis design, traceability and heterogeneity according to the stated question. Do not infer missing analyses or transplant facts from a prior named project.
